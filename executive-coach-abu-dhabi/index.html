Who I work with

Leaders in Abu Dhabi who have just taken on more.

Most of the Abu Dhabi leaders I coach work in government, semi-government entities, energy and utilities, and finance. Many are HR managers and directors. Others have just moved into their first director or general manager role and are finding that what got them there won't carry the new job on its own.

I spent twenty years in HR myself, in regional roles for French, Canadian, American and Japanese companies, and I've worked with organisations in Abu Dhabi including the Department of Finance, ADNOC and TAQA Distribution. I know how decisions get made in these organisations, how much rides on the relationship with your director general or CEO, and how a change programme feels from the middle of it.

Coaching with me is one to one. You bring real situations from your week and we work on them: a stakeholder who won't engage, a team you've inherited, a restructure you have to lead, or a decision about your own next step.

About

Twenty years in HR and leadership development across the GCC, Europe and North America. Based in Dubai for more than twelve.

My story

How I got here.

I started my HR career in Cairo and spent the next two decades inside large companies. I worked for Alstom, Bombardier, Xylem, Daikin and Helmerich & Payne, and my last corporate role was HR Regional Director at Helmerich & Payne. These were French, Canadian, American and Japanese companies, and I covered the Middle East and Africa for them from Dubai.

I know what it's like to be the HR person in a room where nobody asked for your opinion. I know what a restructure feels like from the inside, and what it takes to tell a managing director something they don't want to hear. I also learned what changes how HR is seen, and it's rarely another policy.

In 2025 I left in-house HR. Since then, most of the people who have come to me for coaching have been HR managers and directors. They tell me the same thing: they spend their days developing everyone else, and nobody is doing it for them.

That's who I coach. I work in Arabic and English, I hold the ICF PCC credential with more than 600 coaching hours, and I use Hogan assessments when a clearer picture would help.

Outside coaching, I teach as Adjunct Faculty at PwC Academy Middle East and EY, sit on the judging panels for the CIPD, EHA and HRSE awards, and write about leadership and AI for Harvard Business Review Arabia.

Where the work has taken me

Twenty years, three continents.

I've worked with teams spread across countries and time zones, each with its own idea of what a good manager looks like. That's where I learned to work across cultures.

    Bombardier is Canadian, and Xylem and Helmerich & Payne are American. I held regional HR roles for them in the Middle East and worked closely with their head offices in North America.

Short biography

Hisham Elsaied, FCIPD, PCC, is an executive coach and leadership development consultant based in Dubai. He has more than 20 years of experience in HR and leadership development across the GCC, Europe and North America, including regional HR and talent leadership roles with Alstom, Bombardier, Xylem, Daikin and Helmerich & Payne. He coaches HR managers and directors in Arabic and English and has completed more than 600 hours of executive coaching. Hisham is an ICF Professional Certified Coach, a Chartered Fellow of the CIPD, a Fellow of the Australian HR Institute, a Hogan Certified Assessor, a Cultural Intelligence (CQ) Certified Trainer and an MBTI Certified Practitioner. He is Adjunct Faculty at PwC Academy Middle East and EY, a judge for the CIPD, EHA and HRSE awards, and a contributing author to Harvard Business Review Arabia. He has been named among the Top 100 Global Power Leaders in HR by White Page International.

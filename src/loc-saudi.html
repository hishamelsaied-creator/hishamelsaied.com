Who I work with

Saudi leaders carrying more than they used to.

Organisations in the Kingdom are growing fast, and many leaders now run bigger teams, newer functions and more change than their role was built for. A lot of the Saudi leaders I coach are HR managers and directors. Others have moved into a director or general manager role early and want someone outside the organisation to think with.

My work takes me regularly to Riyadh, Jeddah and Tabuk, and I've worked with organisations in the Kingdom including ZATCA and Tabuk Pharmaceuticals. Before coaching I spent twenty years in HR, in regional roles for multinational companies, so I know the pressure of hiring and developing national talent quickly while keeping the business running.

Most sessions are online, which suits busy diaries in Riyadh or Jeddah. When I'm in the Kingdom we can meet in person. We can work in Arabic, in English, or move between the two.

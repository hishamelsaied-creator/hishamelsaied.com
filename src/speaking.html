Topics

What I speak and run workshops on.

Each topic works as a keynote, a conference masterclass or a half-day or one-day workshop. I shape the content around your audience and the event.

  • Leading people

    The move from manager to leader, influence and stakeholder management, executive presence, and holding people to account. For new and mid-level managers, and the leaders above them.

  • HR capability

    What it takes for HR to be taken seriously by the business: strategic HR, business partnering, workforce planning, and what AI is changing in the HR role. For HR teams and HR leaders. See keynotes on AI and leadership.

  • Talent and assessment

    Spotting and developing future leaders: succession, high potentials, assessment centres, and using Hogan and other assessments well. Plus cultural intelligence for leaders of mixed-nationality teams in the GCC.

Formats

Four formats.

  1. Keynote

    20 to 45 minutes on stage, built around one idea the audience can use.

  2. Masterclass

    60 to 90 minutes at a conference or leadership event, with time for questions and pushback.

  3. Workshop

    A half day or a full day with a leadership or HR group, built on their real situations.

  4. Panel

    As a panellist or moderator. Moderating is the best seat in the room: you talk less and hear more.

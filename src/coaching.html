A first head of HR role. A move from specialist to business partner. A regional remit. A seat on the executive team. The first few months shape how people see you, and it helps to talk them through with someone who has made the same move.

Stuck or under pressure

My approach

How I coach.

I coach to the ICF standard, which means you do the thinking and I don't hand you answers. I've also done your job, so when it helps I'll tell you what I've seen work and what I've seen go wrong.

For career moves I use MOVE™, a framework I built to help people work out where they want to go, check it's realistic, and make the move. I've used it with more than 200 professionals.

Where a clearer picture would help, we can start with a Hogan assessment. It shows how you come across under normal conditions and under pressure, which helps when you've been told you need more presence and nobody has explained why.

We work on real situations from your week, like a meeting you're dreading or a conversation you've been putting off.

How it works

From first message to last session.

  1. You enquire

    Send a few lines about your role and what's on your mind. I reply personally.

  2. We talk

    A first conversation with no obligation. You decide whether I'm the right coach for you.

  3. We set goals

  4. We work

    Regular one-to-one sessions, with a review at the midpoint and at the end.

Questions

What people ask before they start.

Can we work in Arabic?

Yes. I coach in Arabic and English, and many clients move between the two in the same session. Use whichever language you think best in.

Is it confidential?

Yes. I work to the ICF Code of Ethics. What we discuss stays between us, including when your employer is paying, unless the law requires otherwise. We agree at the start what, if anything, is reported back.

Can my organisation pay for it?

Yes. Many clients are sponsored by their employer and others pay themselves. The coaching is the same either way.

How long does an engagement last?

It depends on what you want to change. We agree the length after our first conversation, once I understand your situation.

What does it cost?

Because each engagement is tailored, I share fees after our first conversation.

Do you only coach HR people?

HR managers and directors are who I know best and who I work with most. I also coach senior leaders in government and corporate roles across the GCC.

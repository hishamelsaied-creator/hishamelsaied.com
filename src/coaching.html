My approach

How I coach.

I coach to the ICF standard, which means you do the thinking and I don't hand you answers. I've also done your job, so when it helps I'll tell you what I've seen work and what I've seen go wrong.

For career moves I use MOVE™, a framework I built to help people work out where they want to go, check it's realistic, and make the move. More than 200 professionals have used it to reach leadership roles. See career coaching for HR professionals.

Where a clearer picture would help, we can start with a Hogan assessment. It shows how you come across under normal conditions and under pressure, which helps when you've been told you need more presence and nobody has explained why.

We work on real situations from your week, like a meeting you're dreading or a conversation you've been putting off.

Typical engagements

What three months can look like.

These are typical patterns drawn from many engagements, not individual clients.

New head of HR  ·  semi-government

Brought in after the decisions.

Goals: speak up early on two upcoming business decisions, and report HR's work in business terms.

What usually changes: the leader is asked for a view before decisions are made, and updates to the executive team get shorter and sharper.

HR business partner  ·  banking

Strong delivery, passed over.

Goals: build working relationships with three business heads, and get clear on the next role.

What usually changes: business heads bring the leader in earlier, and the leader has a plan for the next move that their manager supports.

New regional remit  ·  energy

Doing the team's work as well as their own.

Goals: hand over day-to-day decisions, and lead managers across several countries.

What usually changes: the team runs operations without escalating, and the leader gets time back for the bigger work.

Questions

What people ask before they start.

Can we work in Arabic?

Yes. I coach in Arabic and English, and many clients move between the two in the same session. Use whichever language you think best in.

Is it confidential?

Yes. I work to the ICF Code of Ethics. What we discuss stays between us, including when your employer is paying. We agree at the start what, if anything, is reported back.

Can my organisation pay for it?

Yes. Many clients are sponsored by their employer and others pay themselves. The coaching is the same either way. If you're in HR, see how I work with organisations.

How long does an engagement last?

Usually about three months, which is long enough for people around you to notice a change. We agree the length after our first conversation, once I understand your situation.

What does it cost?

Because each engagement is tailored, I share fees after our first conversation.

Do you only coach HR people?

HR managers and directors are who I know best and who I work with most. I also coach senior leaders in government and corporate roles across the GCC.

Outside Dubai? I also coach leaders in Abu Dhabi and leaders in Riyadh and across Saudi Arabia, in person and online.

First 90 days planner

Plan your first three months in a new leadership role. What you write is saved on this device only, and you can save it as a PDF.

Days 1 to 30

Listen and learn

Your manager, each member of the leadership team, your own team, and key people in other functions.
Who does the CEO call before a decision? Whose opinion travels?
Themes that come up again and again.
Days 31 to 60

Early wins

What it is, why the business cares, and who needs to be involved.
Days 61 to 90

Agree and plan

Two or three things. Agree them, then revisit every quarter.
Watch for

Three common traps

  • Copying what worked in your last organisation without asking.
  • Becoming seen as one person's messenger too fast.
  • Disappearing into day-to-day operations.

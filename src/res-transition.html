Leadership transition check

Ten questions for anyone in their first months as a director, head of function or general manager. See where you're on track, and where to focus next.

1I've met each key stakeholder one to one.
2I understand how decisions are really made here, beyond the org chart.
3My manager and I have agreed what success looks like in my first year.
4I know what I should stop doing from my previous role.
5I've formed a view of my team's strengths and gaps, based on evidence.
6I've handed over the work that belongs to someone else now.
7I've picked one or two early wins that the business itself cares about.
8People know what I'm focusing on, and why.
9I protect regular time to think, not just to react.
10I have someone outside my line to think things through with.

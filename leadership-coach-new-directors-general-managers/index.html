Who I work with

You got the job. Now it feels different from the one you were good at.

Most people are promoted because they were strong at the work itself. The new job is mostly about other people: a bigger team, peers who used to be senior to you, and a CEO or board that expects results early.

I spent twenty years in HR, in regional roles for French, Canadian, American and Japanese companies, and finished as a regional HR director. From that seat I saw many leaders make this move up close. I know what tends to trip people up and what helps.

Coaching with me is one to one and practical. You bring what is on your desk this week and we work on it: your first hundred days, a team you didn't choose, a peer who is testing you, or how to disagree with your CEO.

Coaching for organisations
Questions

Common questions.

Is there a leadership coach for new directors and general managers in Dubai?

Yes. Hisham Elsaied is an ICF Professional Certified Coach (PCC) based in Dubai who coaches first-time directors, heads of function and general managers across the UAE and the GCC, in Arabic and English.

When is the best time to start coaching after a promotion?

As early as you can, ideally in the first three months, while relationships and first impressions are still forming. It is still useful later if the job is not going the way you expected.

I'm not in HR. Do you coach leaders in other functions?

Yes. HR leaders are who Hisham works with most, and he also coaches general managers, heads of department and first-time executives in government and corporate roles.

Can my organisation pay for the coaching?

Yes. Many clients are sponsored by their employer. Goals are agreed in a three-way meeting at the start, and the content of sessions stays confidential under the ICF Code of Ethics.

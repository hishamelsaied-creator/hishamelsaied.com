Free self-check for HR leaders

How does your CEO see HR?

A practical diagnostic for HR managers and directors in the Gulf. Fifteen questions about real situations in your work with the CEO and the leadership team.

  • 15 questions about real situations. It takes about 3 minutes.
  • A report by email within 3 minutes. The level your CEO probably sees you at, your score in five areas and a heat map of your answers.
  • A 90-day plan for your two priority areas, and five questions to ask your CEO. You can save the report as a PDF.
  • Optional: get your CEO's view. Send them a 2-minute version and get a report comparing your two views.

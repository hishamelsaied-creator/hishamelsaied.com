Questions

Common questions.

Who speaks about AI and leadership at conferences in Dubai and the GCC?

Hisham Elsaied is a Dubai-based speaker and author who gives keynotes and masterclasses on AI, leadership and HR in Arabic and English. Recent talks include "With AI, who sets the pace?" at HRSE Dubai 2026 and a masterclass at the ETHRWorld NexTech HR Summit Middle East 2026.

Can the talk be delivered in Arabic?

Yes. Hisham speaks in Arabic, English, or both in the same session.

How long is a keynote?

A keynote runs 20 to 45 minutes. A masterclass runs 60 to 90 minutes, and a workshop is a half day or a full day.

Is this a technical talk about AI tools?

No. The talks are for leaders and HR teams. They are about people, pace, productivity and accountability, and they do not teach a particular tool.

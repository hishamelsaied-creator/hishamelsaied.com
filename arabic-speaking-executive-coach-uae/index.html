Why the language matters

Coaching works best in the language you think in.

Many of the leaders I work with run their meetings in English and think through hard decisions in Arabic. In a coaching session you shouldn't have to translate yourself first.

I grew up and started my career in Cairo and have lived in Dubai for more than twelve years. I've worked in Arabic and English my whole career, with government entities and inside multinational companies, so we can use whichever language the moment needs.

It also helps to have a coach who knows how work is done here: how decisions are made, what a manager expects, and what is said in the room and what is left unsaid.
